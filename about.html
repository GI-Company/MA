OUR STORY

BUILT ON
EXPERIENCE.

Owner Tanner Pirkle brings more than 15 years of mechanical experience to Mountain Automotive & Tire. The business originally opened in Cornelia, Georgia in April 2023 and moved to Cleveland in September 2024.

Today, the shop operates eight service bays at 3112 U.S. 129, helping drivers with tire service, maintenance, alignments, brakes, and general repairs.
